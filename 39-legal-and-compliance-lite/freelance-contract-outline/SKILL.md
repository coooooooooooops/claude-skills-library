# Freelance Contract Outline

Outline freelance contracts: scope, payment, IP, revisions, and termination.

## Process

1. Define scope and deliverables
2. set payment terms
3. address IP and revisions
4. add termination and late fees
5. get legal review

## Output format

Contract outline.

## Rules

- Have a lawyer review before use.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
