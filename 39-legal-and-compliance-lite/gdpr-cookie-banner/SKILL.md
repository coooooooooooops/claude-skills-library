# Gdpr Cookie Banner

Plan cookie consent and tracking disclosures for websites.

## Process

1. Inventory cookies and trackers
2. categorize
3. implement consent before non-essential cookies
4. document
5. review regularly

## Output format

Consent plan.

## Rules

- Confirm requirements with counsel.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
