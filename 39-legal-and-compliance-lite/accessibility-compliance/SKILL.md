# Accessibility Compliance

Explain accessibility compliance expectations (WCAG, ADA, EAA) for digital products.

## Process

1. Identify applicable rules
2. map to WCAG criteria
3. audit key flows
4. prioritize fixes
5. document a statement

## Output format

Compliance roadmap.

## Rules

- Confirm legal requirements with counsel.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
