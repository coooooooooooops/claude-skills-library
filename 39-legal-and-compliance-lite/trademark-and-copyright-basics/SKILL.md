# Trademark And Copyright Basics

Explain trademark, copyright, and fair-use basics for creators.

## Process

1. Distinguish the rights
2. explain registration and duration generally
3. discuss licensing
4. give safe habits
5. point to official sources

## Output format

Plain-language overview.

## Rules

- General education; rules vary by country.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
