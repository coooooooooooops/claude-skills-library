# Nda Explainer

Explain NDAs: scope, term, exclusions, and red flags.

## Process

1. Identify parties and purpose
2. summarize what is confidential
3. check term and exclusions
4. flag one-sided clauses
5. list questions

## Output format

Plain-English summary.

## Rules

- Not legal advice.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
