# Reading Notes

Turn reading into usable knowledge.

## Process

1. Capture the thesis and structure.
2. List key ideas in your own words.
3. Connect to what the user already knows.
4. Write action items and questions.

## Output format

Structured notes.

## Rules

- Quote sparingly and attribute.
- Do not reproduce long copyrighted passages.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
