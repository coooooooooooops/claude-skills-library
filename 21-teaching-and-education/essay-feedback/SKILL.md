# Essay Feedback

Give feedback on student essays on thesis, evidence, structure, and style.

## Process

1. Identify thesis and argument
2. assess evidence
3. review structure and transitions
4. note style issues
5. give three priority revisions

## Output format

Feedback report.

## Rules

- Praise first, then prioritize improvements.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
