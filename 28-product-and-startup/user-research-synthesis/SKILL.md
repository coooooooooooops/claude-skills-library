# User Research Synthesis

Synthesize interviews and feedback into themes and decisions.

## Process

1. Tag raw notes
2. cluster themes
3. count frequency and intensity
4. extract insights
5. recommend actions

## Output format

Insights report.

## Rules

- Keep quotes tied to evidence.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
