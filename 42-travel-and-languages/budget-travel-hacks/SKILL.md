# Budget Travel Hacks

Travel cheaper with flexible dates, hostels, transit, and local food.

## Process

1. Set total budget
2. find cheap flights with flexibility
3. pick affordable lodging
4. use local transit
5. track spend

## Output format

Budget plan.

## Rules

- Verify current prices.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
