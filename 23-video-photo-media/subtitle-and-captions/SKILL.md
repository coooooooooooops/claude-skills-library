# Subtitle And Captions

Create accurate captions and subtitles with timing and style rules.

## Process

1. Transcribe
2. split into readable lines
3. time to speech
4. follow reading speed
5. proofread and export SRT

## Output format

Caption file guidance.

## Rules

- Keep lines short and readable.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
