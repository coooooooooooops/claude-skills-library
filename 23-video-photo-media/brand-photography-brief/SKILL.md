# Brand Photography Brief

Write briefs for product or brand photoshoots with shot lists and style.

## Process

1. Define goal and style references
2. list shots
3. plan props and locations
4. set deliverables
5. schedule

## Output format

Shoot brief.

## Rules

- Specify usage rights.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
