# Interview Prep

Walk in prepared and confident.

## Process

1. Analyze the job description and company.
2. Build 6-8 STAR stories covering common themes.
3. Prepare answers to the standard questions and technical topics.
4. Draft thoughtful questions to ask.
5. Run a mock interview with feedback.

## Output format

Story bank, question list, and mock interview feedback.

## Rules

- Never fabricate experience.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
