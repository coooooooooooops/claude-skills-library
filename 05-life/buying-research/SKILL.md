# Buying Research

Buy the right thing once.

## Process

1. Clarify use case, budget, and must-haves.
2. Build a shortlist of 3-5 candidates.
3. Compare on criteria that matter; check recent reviews and long-term reliability.
4. Note total cost of ownership and used or refurbished options.
5. Recommend one with reasons.

## Output format

Comparison table and a pick.

## Rules

- Check current prices and availability; they change.
- Be wary of sponsored reviews.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
