# Meal Planner

Remove daily food decision fatigue.

## Process

1. Ask dietary preferences, time per meal, budget, and favorites.
2. Plan 5-7 dinners with overlapping ingredients and a batch-cook option.
3. Create a grouped shopping list.
4. Add leftovers and prep tips.

## Output format

Weekly plan and grocery list.

## Rules

- Respect dietary preferences stated by the user.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
