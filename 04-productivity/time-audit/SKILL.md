# Time Audit

Make time visible so it can be redirected.

## Process

1. Record a typical week in categories.
2. Compare actual vs. desired allocation.
3. Find top time sinks and low-value commitments.
4. Propose three reallocations and one thing to stop.
5. Review in two weeks.

## Output format

Time allocation table and three changes.

## Rules

- Estimates are fine; patterns matter more than precision.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
