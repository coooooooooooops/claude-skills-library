# Weekly Review

Close the week, clear the decks, and set direction.

## Process

1. Collect: calendar, tasks, notes, and inbox.
2. List wins and what drained energy.
3. Capture open loops; decide do, delegate, schedule, or drop.
4. Pick the top 3 outcomes for next week.
5. Time-block them in the calendar.

## Output format

One-page review with Top 3 and a schedule sketch.

## Rules

- Keep it under 30 minutes.
- Drop tasks without guilt.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
