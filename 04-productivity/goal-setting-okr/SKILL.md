# Goal Setting Okr

Make goals measurable and actionable.

## Process

1. Clarify the outcome and why it matters.
2. Write 1-3 objectives with 2-4 measurable key results each.
3. Break key results into monthly milestones and weekly actions.
4. Define leading indicators and a review cadence.
5. Identify obstacles and if-then plans.

## Output format

OKR table plus a 12-week plan.

## Rules

- Key results measure outcomes, not tasks.
- Limit active goals to what can truly be done.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
