# Meeting Notes

Leave every meeting with clarity.

## Process

1. Extract attendees, topic, and decisions.
2. List action items with owner and due date.
3. Flag open questions and risks.
4. Write a 5-line summary for those who missed it.

## Output format

Summary, decisions, action table, and open questions.

## Rules

- Do not invent owners or dates; mark TBD.
- Keep it scannable.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
