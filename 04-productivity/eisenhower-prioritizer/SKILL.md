# Eisenhower Prioritizer

Cut through noise to what matters.

## Process

1. Collect every task.
2. Classify into the four quadrants.
3. Delete or delegate the low-value items.
4. Schedule the important-not-urgent work first.
5. Identify recurring urgent items to systematize.

## Output format

Four-quadrant list with next actions.

## Rules

- Be ruthless about 'delete'.
- Protect time for important-not-urgent work.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
