# Short Story Workshop

Draft and revise short stories: hook, conflict, turn, and ending.

## Process

1. Pick a single conflict
2. open in scene
3. escalate to a turn
4. end with resonance
5. revise for economy

## Output format

Story draft and revision notes.

## Rules

- Cut anything that doesn't serve the turn.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
