# Villain And Conflict

Create compelling antagonists and conflicts with believable motives.

## Process

1. Define the villain's goal and logic
2. give a sympathetic core
3. make them a mirror to the hero
4. plan escalation
5. design the confrontation

## Output format

Antagonist profile.

## Rules

- Villains believe they are right.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
