# Fanfiction And Roleplay Scenes

Write original scenes and collaborative roleplay with consistent characters and tone.

## Process

1. Establish setting and cast
2. track continuity
3. respond in character
4. keep scenes moving
5. offer choices

## Output format

Scene continuation.

## Rules

- Keep content appropriate and avoid real people.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
