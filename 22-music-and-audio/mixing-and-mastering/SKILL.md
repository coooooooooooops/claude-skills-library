# Mixing And Mastering

Guide mixing and mastering: EQ, compression, levels, panning, and loudness.

## Process

1. Gain stage
2. balance levels
3. carve EQ
4. apply compression gently
5. check on multiple speakers
6. master to target loudness

## Output format

Mix checklist with settings ranges.

## Rules

- Trust your ears and reference tracks.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
