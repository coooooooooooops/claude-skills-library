# Beat Making

Build drum patterns and beats across genres.

## Process

1. Choose tempo and genre
2. place kick and snare
3. add hats and percussion
4. add swing and variation
5. arrange sections

## Output format

Pattern grid.

## Rules

- Space and groove beat complexity.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
