# Print On Demand Design

Create print-on-demand designs and mockups with niche research.

## Process

1. Pick a niche
2. research trends
3. create original designs
4. make mockups
5. list with keywords

## Output format

Design and listing plan.

## Rules

- Use original art; avoid trademarked content.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
