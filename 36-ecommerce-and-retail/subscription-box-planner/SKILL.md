# Subscription Box Planner

Plan a subscription box business: curation, costs, churn, and logistics.

## Process

1. Define niche
2. model per-box cost and shipping
3. plan sourcing
4. set pricing
5. plan retention and churn

## Output format

Business plan outline.

## Rules

- Model churn conservatively.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
