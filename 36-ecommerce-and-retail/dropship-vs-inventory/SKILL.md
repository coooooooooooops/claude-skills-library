# Dropship Vs Inventory

Compare dropshipping, print-on-demand, wholesale, and making your own products.

## Process

1. List margins, risk and control per model
2. estimate startup cost
3. consider shipping times and returns
4. recommend a starting model

## Output format

Comparison table.

## Rules

- Include returns and support costs.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
