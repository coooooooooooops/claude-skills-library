# Product Photography Setup

Set up DIY product photography with lighting, backdrops, angles, and editing.

## Process

1. Choose backdrop and light source
2. shoot multiple angles and details
3. keep consistent style
4. edit exposure and crop
5. export per marketplace

## Output format

Photo setup guide.

## Rules

- Photos must match the real item.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
