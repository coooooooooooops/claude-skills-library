# Marketplace Fee Calculator

Calculate net profit after marketplace fees, shipping, and returns across eBay, Etsy, Amazon, and others.

## Process

1. Collect sale price, fees and shipping
2. add payment and ad costs
3. account for returns
4. compute net and margin
5. set a minimum price

## Output format

Profit table.

## Rules

- Check current fee schedules.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
