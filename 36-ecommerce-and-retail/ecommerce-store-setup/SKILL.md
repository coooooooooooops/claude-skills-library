# Ecommerce Store Setup

Plan an online store: platform choice, catalog, payments, shipping, taxes, and policies.

## Process

1. Choose platform
2. structure catalog
3. set up payments and shipping rates
4. add policy pages
5. test checkout

## Output format

Setup checklist.

## Rules

- Verify tax and legal requirements for your region.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
