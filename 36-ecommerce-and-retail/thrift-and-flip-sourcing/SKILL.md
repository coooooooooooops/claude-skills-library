# Thrift And Flip Sourcing

Source resale inventory from thrift stores, estate sales, and auctions with margin math.

## Process

1. Set categories and minimum margin
2. check sold comps before buying
3. inspect condition
4. track costs
5. list quickly

## Output format

Sourcing playbook.

## Rules

- Only buy what you can price from sold data.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
