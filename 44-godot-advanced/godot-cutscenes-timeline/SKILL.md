# Godot Cutscenes Timeline

Create cutscenes with AnimationPlayer, tracks, and camera control.

## Process

1. Storyboard beats
2. animate with tracks and method calls
3. control camera and audio
4. allow skipping
5. test

## Output format

Cutscene setup.

## Rules

- Always let players skip.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
