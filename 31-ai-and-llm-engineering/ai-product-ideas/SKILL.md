# Ai Product Ideas

Brainstorm AI product ideas from workflows with repetitive judgment or text work.

## Process

1. List painful workflows
2. check data availability
3. judge feasibility
4. define human-in-the-loop
5. validate cheaply

## Output format

Idea shortlist.

## Rules

- Pick problems where errors are tolerable.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
