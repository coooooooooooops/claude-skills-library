# Chatbot Conversation Design

Design chatbot conversations: persona, flows, fallbacks, and handoff.

## Process

1. Define scope and persona
2. map key flows
3. write fallback and clarification behavior
4. add human handoff
5. review transcripts

## Output format

Conversation design doc.

## Rules

- State clearly what the bot can't do.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
