# Voice Assistant Design

Design voice interfaces: wake words, turn-taking, speech recognition, and TTS.

## Process

1. Define use cases
2. choose ASR and TTS
3. handle interruptions
4. confirm risky actions
5. test with accents and noise

## Output format

Voice architecture.

## Rules

- Keep responses short for speech.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
