# Backup And Recovery

Design backup and disaster-recovery plans with RPO/RTO, testing, and offsite copies.

## Process

1. Define RPO and RTO
2. choose backup method and frequency
3. keep 3-2-1 copies
4. automate and encrypt
5. schedule restore tests

## Output format

Backup policy and restore procedure.

## Rules

- A backup is not real until a restore has been tested.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
