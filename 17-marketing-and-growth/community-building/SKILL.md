# Community Building

Build and manage online communities on Discord, forums, or social.

## Process

1. Define purpose and rules
2. seed with a core group
3. create rituals and onboarding
4. moderate consistently
5. measure engagement

## Output format

Community plan.

## Rules

- Set and enforce clear conduct rules.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
