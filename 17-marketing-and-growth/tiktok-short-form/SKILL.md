# Tiktok Short Form

Create short-form video concepts with hooks, pacing, captions, and trends.

## Process

1. Choose a repeatable format
2. write a 3-second hook
3. script beats and captions
4. add a call to action
5. batch produce

## Output format

Script ideas list.

## Rules

- Hook within the first seconds.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
