# Product Hunt Launch

Plan a product launch on Product Hunt or similar: assets, timing, outreach, and follow-up.

## Process

1. Prepare tagline, gallery and first comment
2. build supporter list ethically
3. choose launch day
4. respond to comments all day
5. follow up after

## Output format

Launch checklist and timeline.

## Rules

- Do not spam or buy votes.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
