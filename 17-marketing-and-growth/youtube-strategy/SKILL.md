# Youtube Strategy

Plan YouTube channels and videos: niche, titles, thumbnails, hooks, and retention.

## Process

1. Define niche and viewer
2. research topics
3. craft title and thumbnail concepts
4. script a strong first 30 seconds
5. review retention analytics

## Output format

Channel and video plan.

## Rules

- Do not mislead with titles or thumbnails.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
