# Growth Experiments

Design growth experiments with hypotheses, ICE scoring, and learning loops.

## Process

1. List funnel stages
2. brainstorm ideas
3. score by impact, confidence and ease
4. run small tests
5. document learnings

## Output format

Experiment backlog.

## Rules

- Run one experiment per variable.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
