# Content Repurposing

Turn one piece of content into many formats across channels.

## Process

1. Pick a core asset
2. extract key ideas
3. adapt to each channel
4. schedule over weeks
5. link back to the original

## Output format

Repurposing map.

## Rules

- Adapt, don't just paste.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
