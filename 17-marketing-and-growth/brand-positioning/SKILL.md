# Brand Positioning

Define brand positioning, voice, and messaging pillars.

## Process

1. Identify audience and category
2. find the differentiated claim
3. write positioning statement
4. define voice traits
5. create messaging pillars

## Output format

Positioning one-pager.

## Rules

- Positioning must be specific enough to exclude someone.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
