# Seo Content Strategy

Plan SEO content with keyword research, intent mapping, topic clusters, and briefs.

## Process

1. Research keywords and intent
2. build topic clusters
3. prioritize by difficulty and value
4. write briefs
5. plan internal links

## Output format

Content calendar and briefs.

## Rules

- Write for the searcher first.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
