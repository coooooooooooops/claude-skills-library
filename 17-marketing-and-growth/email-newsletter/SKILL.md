# Email Newsletter

Write and structure email newsletters and drip sequences with strong subject lines.

## Process

1. Define purpose and audience
2. write subject line options
3. structure with one main call to action
4. plan a sequence
5. track opens and clicks

## Output format

Email copy and sequence plan.

## Rules

- Respect unsubscribe and consent rules.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
