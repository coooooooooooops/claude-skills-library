# Competitor Analysis

Know the field, find the gap.

## Process

1. List direct and indirect competitors.
2. Compare features, pricing, audience, and messaging.
3. Find unserved needs and weaknesses.
4. Recommend differentiation.

## Output format

Competitor matrix and gap summary.

## Rules

- Verify claims with current sources.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
