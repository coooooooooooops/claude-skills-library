# Landing Page Copy

Clear promise, strong proof, simple action.

## Process

1. Define audience and the one action.
2. Write headline and subhead around the main benefit.
3. Add benefits, social proof, and FAQ for objections.
4. Craft CTA text.
5. Suggest layout order.

## Output format

Section-by-section page copy.

## Rules

- No false claims or fake testimonials.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
