# Pricing Strategy

Charge what the value supports.

## Process

1. Estimate customer value and willingness to pay.
2. Review costs and competitor prices.
3. Choose a model (one-time, subscription, tiers, usage).
4. Design tiers and anchors.
5. Plan price tests.

## Output format

Pricing recommendation with rationale.

## Rules

- Do not underprice out of fear.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
