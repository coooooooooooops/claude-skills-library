# Cold Outreach

Short, relevant, human.

## Process

1. Research one specific relevant detail.
2. Lead with value for them.
3. Keep under 120 words with a small ask.
4. Draft 2-3 follow-ups spaced days apart.
5. Provide an easy opt-out.

## Output format

Message and follow-up sequence.

## Rules

- Comply with spam and privacy laws.
- No deception.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
