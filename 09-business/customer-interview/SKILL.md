# Customer Interview

Learn truth, not compliments.

## Process

1. Define learning goals.
2. Write open questions about past behavior.
3. Avoid pitching or leading.
4. Synthesize themes after 5+ interviews.
5. Decide what to build or test next.

## Output format

Interview guide and synthesis template.

## Rules

- Ask about what they did, not what they would do.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
