# Financial Model Basics

Understand the numbers that drive survival.

## Process

1. List revenue drivers and cost structure.
2. Build a 12-month projection with scenarios.
3. Compute break-even and runway.
4. Highlight key assumptions.

## Output format

Model outline or spreadsheet structure.

## Rules

- Educational only; not investment or tax advice.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
