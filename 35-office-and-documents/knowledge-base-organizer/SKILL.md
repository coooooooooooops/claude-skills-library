# Knowledge Base Organizer

Organize team knowledge bases: taxonomy, naming, ownership, and cleanup.

## Process

1. Audit content
2. define structure and naming
3. assign owners
4. archive stale pages
5. set review cadence

## Output format

KB structure plan.

## Rules

- Every page needs an owner.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
