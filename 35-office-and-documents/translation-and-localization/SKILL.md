# Translation And Localization

Translate and localize text with tone, idioms, and cultural adaptation.

## Process

1. Identify audience and region
2. translate meaning not words
3. adapt idioms and formats
4. keep terminology consistent
5. back-check

## Output format

Translated text with notes.

## Rules

- Flag ambiguous source phrases.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
