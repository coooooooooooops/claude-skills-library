# Meeting Agenda Builder

Build agendas with goals, timeboxes, and pre-reads.

## Process

1. State purpose
2. list topics with owners and times
3. attach pre-reads
4. end with decisions and actions

## Output format

Agenda.

## Rules

- Each item needs a desired outcome.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
