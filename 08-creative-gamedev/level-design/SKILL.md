# Level Design

Guide the player through memorable spaces.

## Process

1. Define the level's purpose and new mechanic.
2. Sketch layout with safe areas, challenges, and landmarks.
3. Teach, test, twist: introduce, practice, then subvert.
4. Plan pacing peaks and rest.
5. Add secrets and replay value.

## Output format

Layout description and beat sheet.

## Rules

- Teach through play, not text.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
