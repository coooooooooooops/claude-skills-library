# Worldbuilding

Create worlds that feel lived-in.

## Process

1. Define tone and central conflict.
2. Create geography, then cultures that fit it.
3. Add factions with goals and tension.
4. Write key history and secrets.
5. Provide hooks for stories.

## Output format

World bible with hooks.

## Rules

- Depth over breadth; make details serve the story.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
