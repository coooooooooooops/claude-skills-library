# Privacy Policy Drafter

Draft plain-language privacy policies and data inventories for apps.

## Process

1. Inventory data collected
2. define purposes and retention
3. list third parties
4. describe user rights
5. draft the policy

## Output format

Draft policy and data map.

## Rules

- Draft for review by a qualified lawyer; laws vary by region.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
