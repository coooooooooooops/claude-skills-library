# Encryption Basics

Explain encryption, hashing, TLS, and key management at a practical level.

## Process

1. Distinguish hashing, symmetric and asymmetric
2. pick vetted libraries
3. manage keys safely
4. use TLS correctly
5. avoid homemade schemes

## Output format

Practical explainer.

## Rules

- Never invent your own cryptography.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
