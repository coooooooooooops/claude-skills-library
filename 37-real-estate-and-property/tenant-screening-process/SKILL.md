# Tenant Screening Process

Design a fair tenant screening process with consistent criteria.

## Process

1. Define written criteria
2. follow fair-housing rules
3. verify income and references
4. apply criteria equally
5. document decisions

## Output format

Screening checklist.

## Rules

- Follow fair-housing laws strictly.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
