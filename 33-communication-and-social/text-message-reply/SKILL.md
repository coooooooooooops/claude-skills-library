# Text Message Reply

Draft thoughtful replies to texts and messages with the right tone.

## Process

1. Read the message and relationship
2. choose tone
3. draft two options
4. keep it short
5. check it can't be misread

## Output format

Reply options.

## Rules

- Keep it genuine.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
