# Car Maintenance Basics

Explain routine car maintenance: fluids, tires, filters, and diagnostic codes.

## Process

1. Review the service schedule
2. check fluids and tires
3. read OBD codes
4. decide DIY versus shop
5. keep records

## Output format

Maintenance schedule.

## Rules

- Use jack stands, never just a jack, and defer safety-critical work to professionals.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
