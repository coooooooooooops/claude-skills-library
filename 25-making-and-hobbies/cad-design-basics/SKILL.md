# Cad Design Basics

Design parts in CAD (Fusion, FreeCAD, Onshape) with sketches, constraints, and tolerances.

## Process

1. Sketch with constraints
2. extrude and pattern
3. add fillets
4. set tolerances for fit
5. export STL or STEP

## Output format

Modeling steps.

## Rules

- Fully constrain sketches.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
