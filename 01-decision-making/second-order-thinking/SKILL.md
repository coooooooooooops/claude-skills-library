# Second Order Thinking

Ask 'and then what?' repeatedly to expose consequences others miss.

## Process

1. State the action and its intended first-order effect.
2. For each stakeholder, list how their behavior or incentives change in response.
3. Chain at least three levels: 'then what?' for every notable effect.
4. Identify reinforcing and balancing feedback loops and time delays.
5. Highlight effects that are good short-term but bad long-term (and the reverse).
6. Suggest tweaks that keep the upside while dampening the worst downstream effects.

## Output format

A consequence tree (indented list) with a short 'what changes my mind' section.

## Rules

- Keep each branch concrete and plausible; avoid doom spirals.
- Label speculation with confidence levels.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
