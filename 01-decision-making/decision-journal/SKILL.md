# Decision Journal

Capture decisions in a format that makes later review honest and useful.

## Process

1. Record date, decision, options considered, and the chosen option.
2. Write the key reasons and the information available at the time.
3. State expected outcomes with probabilities and a confidence level.
4. Set a review date and the metrics to check.
5. At review time, compare outcome vs. expectation and separate decision quality from luck.

## Output format

A reusable markdown journal template filled in for the decision, plus the review prompt.

## Rules

- Judge the process, not just the result.
- Keep entries short enough that the habit sticks.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
