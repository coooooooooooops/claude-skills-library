# Bias Check

Spot and neutralize the biases most likely to distort this specific decision.

## Process

1. Summarize the reasoning so far and the preferred conclusion.
2. Scan for the common biases; list only those with real evidence in the user's own words.
3. For each: quote the signal, explain the distortion, and give a debiasing move (e.g. outside view, reference class, 10-10-10, premortem).
4. Re-run the decision after applying the debiasing moves and report whether the answer changes.

## Output format

Short bias report (signal, effect, fix) and a revised conclusion.

## Rules

- Do not label biases without evidence.
- Be kind: biases are normal, not character flaws.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
