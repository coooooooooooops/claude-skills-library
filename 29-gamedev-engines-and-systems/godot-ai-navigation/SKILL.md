# Godot Ai Navigation

Add Godot enemy AI: state machines, NavigationAgent, perception, and behavior trees.

## Process

1. Define states
2. use NavigationAgent3D or 2D
3. add vision and hearing checks
4. avoid crowding
5. debug visually

## Output format

AI scripts.

## Rules

- Keep AI states few and clear.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
