# Steam Page And Launch

Prepare a Steam or itch.io page: capsule art, trailer, description, wishlists, and launch timing.

## Process

1. Write short hook
2. create capsule and screenshots
3. cut a 30-second trailer
4. build wishlists early
5. choose release date

## Output format

Store page checklist.

## Rules

- Show gameplay in the first seconds of the trailer.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
