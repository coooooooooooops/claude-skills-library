# Headline And Hook

Earn attention honestly.

## Process

1. Clarify the core promise and audience.
2. Write 10+ options using different formulas (benefit, curiosity, number, contrast).
3. Rank the top 3 and say why.
4. Suggest an A/B test.

## Output format

Ranked list of options.

## Rules

- No clickbait that the content cannot deliver.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
