# Storytelling

Make people feel the point.

## Process

1. Pick the core message.
2. Choose a structure (three-act, hero's journey, problem-solution).
3. Define the character, want, obstacle, and change.
4. Use concrete sensory detail and a clear ending.
5. Trim ruthlessly.

## Output format

Story draft and a one-line moral.

## Rules

- Show, don't tell.
- Do not fabricate facts in non-fiction.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
