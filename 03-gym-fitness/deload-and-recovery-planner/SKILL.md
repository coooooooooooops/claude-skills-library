# Deload And Recovery Planner

Recover so progress continues.

## Process

1. Check signs of accumulated fatigue: performance drop, poor sleep, persistent soreness.
2. Choose deload type: reduce volume 40-50%, or load ~10% less, or both for a week.
3. Review sleep, food, and rest-day habits.
4. Plan the return to normal training.
5. Set a recurring deload cadence.

## Output format

A one-week deload plan and recovery checklist.

## Rules

- Keep the deload week genuinely easy.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
