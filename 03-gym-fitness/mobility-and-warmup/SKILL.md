# Mobility And Warmup

Prepare the body to move well and feel better.

## Process

1. Identify the session or the tight area.
2. Build a 5-10 minute warm-up: raise temperature, activate, ramp-up sets.
3. Add targeted mobility drills with time or reps.
4. Offer a 10-minute daily routine for posture or stiffness.
5. Explain what to feel and what to stop.

## Output format

Timed routine with drill names, durations, and cues.

## Rules

- Keep movements controlled and comfortable.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
