# Negotiation For Sellers

Prepare commercial negotiations: value anchors, concessions, and trades.

## Process

1. Define goals and limits
2. anchor on value
3. plan trades not discounts
4. prepare for procurement
5. document agreement

## Output format

Negotiation plan.

## Rules

- Trade concessions; never give them away.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
