# Churn Analysis

Analyze why customers leave and design retention actions.

## Process

1. Segment churners
2. find common signals
3. interview leavers
4. prioritize fixes
5. design win-back and early-warning

## Output format

Churn report and action plan.

## Rules

- Look for leading indicators, not only exits.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
