# Faq And Help Center

Build FAQs and help center articles that deflect tickets.

## Process

1. Mine tickets for top questions
2. write short task-based answers
3. add screenshots
4. structure by topic
5. review quarterly

## Output format

Help center outline and articles.

## Rules

- Write for someone frustrated and in a hurry.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
