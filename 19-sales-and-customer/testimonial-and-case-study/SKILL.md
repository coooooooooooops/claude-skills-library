# Testimonial And Case Study

Collect testimonials and write case studies with measurable results.

## Process

1. Select a successful customer
2. interview about before and after
3. gather metrics
4. structure as challenge, solution, results
5. get approval

## Output format

Case study draft.

## Rules

- Only publish with permission and real numbers.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
