# Sales Discovery Call

Plan and run sales discovery calls with questions that uncover pain, budget, and decision process.

## Process

1. Research the prospect
2. prepare open questions
3. uncover problem and impact
4. map decision makers
5. agree on next steps

## Output format

Call guide and notes template.

## Rules

- Listen more than you pitch.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
