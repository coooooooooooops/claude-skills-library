# Fact Check

Separate what is known from what is claimed.

## Process

1. Isolate the specific claim.
2. Find primary and reliable secondary sources.
3. Check dates, context, and quotes.
4. Rate: supported, partly supported, unsupported, or unverifiable.
5. State confidence and what could change it.

## Output format

Verdict with sources and caveats.

## Rules

- Do not overstate certainty.
- Prefer primary sources.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
