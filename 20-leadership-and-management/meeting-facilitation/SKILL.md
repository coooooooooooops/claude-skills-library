# Meeting Facilitation

Run effective meetings with agendas, timeboxes, decisions, and follow-up.

## Process

1. Define the purpose and decision
2. invite only needed people
3. timebox agenda
4. capture decisions and owners
5. send recap

## Output format

Agenda and facilitation guide.

## Rules

- No agenda, no meeting.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
