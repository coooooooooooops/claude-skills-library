# Strategy One Pager

Write a concise strategy: diagnosis, guiding policy, and coherent actions.

## Process

1. Diagnose the core challenge
2. set guiding policy
3. list coordinated actions
4. define what you won't do
5. set success measures

## Output format

One-page strategy.

## Rules

- Strategy includes saying no.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
