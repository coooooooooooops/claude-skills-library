# Onboarding Flow

Design onboarding that gets users to their first success fast.

## Process

1. Define the aha moment
2. remove steps to reach it
3. use progressive disclosure
4. add checklists and empty-state guidance
5. measure activation

## Output format

Onboarding flow and metrics.

## Rules

- Optimize time to first value.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
