# Design Critique

Give constructive visual design critique on hierarchy, spacing, alignment, and consistency.

## Process

1. State goals
2. assess hierarchy and focal point
3. check spacing and alignment
4. review consistency
5. give prioritized actionable fixes

## Output format

Critique with top 5 changes.

## Rules

- Be specific and kind.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
