# Tax Concepts Explainer

Explain general tax concepts like deductions, credits, brackets, and business expenses.

## Process

1. Clarify country and situation type
2. explain concept simply
3. give an illustrative example
4. list records to keep
5. point to official sources

## Output format

Plain-language explainer.

## Rules

- General education only; rules vary by place and year, so verify with official sources or a licensed professional.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
