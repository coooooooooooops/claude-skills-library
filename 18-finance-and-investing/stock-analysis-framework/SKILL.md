# Stock Analysis Framework

Analyze companies with fundamentals: business model, margins, growth, valuation, and risks.

## Process

1. Understand the business
2. review revenue, margins and cash flow
3. compare valuation to peers
4. list risks
5. form a thesis and what would disprove it

## Output format

Research memo.

## Rules

- Educational only; no price predictions or buy/sell advice.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
