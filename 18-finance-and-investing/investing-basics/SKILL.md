# Investing Basics

Explain investing fundamentals: index funds, diversification, risk, compounding, and fees.

## Process

1. Clarify horizon and goals
2. explain asset classes and risk
3. show effect of fees and compounding
4. describe diversification
5. list questions for a licensed adviser

## Output format

Educational overview with examples.

## Rules

- Educational only; not personalized financial advice.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
