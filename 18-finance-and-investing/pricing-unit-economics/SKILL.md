# Pricing Unit Economics

Compute unit economics for products: COGS, margins, fees, and break-even volumes.

## Process

1. List per-unit revenue and costs
2. include fees and returns
3. compute margin and break-even
4. test price changes
5. flag weak points

## Output format

Unit economics table.

## Rules

- Include all hidden costs.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
