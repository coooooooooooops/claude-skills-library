# Rent Vs Buy Analysis

Compare renting and buying a home with total cost, opportunity cost, and sensitivity.

## Process

1. Collect prices, rates, fees and rent
2. model total cost over time
3. include opportunity cost
4. run sensitivity on appreciation
5. summarize breakeven

## Output format

Comparison table and breakeven year.

## Rules

- Educational only; assumptions drive the answer.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
