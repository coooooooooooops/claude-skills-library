# Freelancer Finances

Organize freelance and side-business finances: invoicing, quarterly set-asides, expenses, and rates.

## Process

1. Set a rate from target income and hours
2. create invoice template
3. separate business account
4. track expenses
5. plan set-asides

## Output format

Finance setup checklist.

## Rules

- General guidance; confirm obligations locally.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
