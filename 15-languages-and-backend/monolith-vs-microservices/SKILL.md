# Monolith Vs Microservices

Decide between monolith, modular monolith, and microservices based on team and scale.

## Process

1. Assess team size, scale and deployment needs
2. list coupling and data ownership
3. compare operational cost
4. recommend and define split triggers

## Output format

Recommendation with tradeoffs.

## Rules

- Default to a modular monolith until proven otherwise.
- Ask at most one or two clarifying questions when key inputs are missing; otherwise state assumptions and proceed.
- Match the user's level and tone; keep the response as short as the task allows.
